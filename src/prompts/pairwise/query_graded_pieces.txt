The query piece A is given as labelled fields:
  piece_title: the actual title of the piece
  movement   : (optional) the specific movement or section [if any]
  work       : (optional) the larger work A belongs to (a suite, cycle, set, opera/musical, collection)
  book       : (optional) the exam album, anthology, or edition A was printed in
This information is collected from noisy PDF parsing and OCR, so, while the information is generally complete, values may not be in the correct fields.
For instance, the work title may be in the book field, or the movement in the piece_title. Therefore, the field labels are for guidance only.
